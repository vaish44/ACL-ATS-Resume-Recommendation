ATS_Application - Resume Recommendation
==========================

ATS_Application helps you rank candidates by what they have actually built, not by keywords.
Candidates upload resumes and apply to jobs. Hirers post jobs and see ranked,
explained scores for every applicant.


BEFORE THE FIRST START (one time only)
--------------------------------------
1. Python must be installed on the computer.
   - Windows: download it from https://www.python.org/downloads/
     IMPORTANT: on the first installer screen, tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
   - Mac: download it from https://www.python.org/downloads/ and install it normally.
2. The first start needs an internet connection. It downloads the app's
   components, which takes about 2-5 minutes. Later starts take a few seconds.


HOW TO START
------------
Windows : double-click  run.bat
Mac     : open Terminal, drag the  run.sh  file into the window, press Enter
Linux   : run  ./run.sh  in a terminal

Your browser opens automatically at  http://127.0.0.1:1114
Keep the black window open while you use ATS_Application. Close it to stop ATS_Application.


FIRST STEPS IN THE APP
----------------------
1. Click "Create an account" and choose "Hirer".
2. Click "Post a job" and paste the full job description.
3. Candidates create their own "Candidate" accounts, upload a resume and apply.
4. Open your job and click "Rankings" to see candidates sorted by score.
   - Click "View evaluation" for the full breakdown and skill evidence.
   - Tick 2-4 candidates and click "Compare" to see them side by side.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Python was not found": install Python (see above) and tick "Add to PATH".
- "AI is overloaded, try again in a minute": the AI service is busy. Wait a
  minute, then click "Run AI analysis" on the job page, or "Re-evaluate" on the
  evaluation page. Your data is saved.
- The first start failed partway: delete the "venv" folder and start again.
- The page will not open: make sure the black window is still open, then go to
  http://127.0.0.1:1114 in your browser.

All data (accounts, jobs, resumes) is saved in the "data" folder on this computer.
Back up that folder to keep your data.

AI settings are in env_app.py. Only change them if IT asks you to.
